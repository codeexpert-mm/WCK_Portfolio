README: Resume Placeholder

This is a placeholder file for your resume.pdf

To replace this with your actual resume:

1. Save your resume as "resume.pdf"
2. Place it in the public/ directory (same location as this file)
3. Delete this README-resume.txt file

The resume download button in your About section will automatically work once you place your resume.pdf file here.

File path: /public/resume.pdf
Download URL: http://localhost:3000/resume.pdf (local) or https://yourdomain.com/resume.pdf (production)

Tips for your resume:
- Keep it 1-2 pages
- Use PDF format for best compatibility
- Include your contact information
- Highlight your technical skills
- Include relevant projects and experience
- Use consistent formatting
- Proofread carefully
